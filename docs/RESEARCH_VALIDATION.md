# Reproducible research validation

Research → Validation freezes a hypothesis, one strategy version, an immutable dataset fingerprint, engine version, capital and allocation policy. Keep at least 252 development sessions and 63 final-test sessions, with earlier signal warm-up history in the same snapshot. A meaningful hypothesis is recorded before the final result.

Development produces eight trials: the full development baseline; three consecutive, disjoint forward evaluation windows with expanding prior history; higher slippage (15 rather than 5 bps, with the same 1 bp fee); a 3% stock cap; 15 positions; and monthly rather than weekly rebalancing. Forward windows keep the recipe fixed. They do not fit an optimizer, and their cash-started returns are not stitched into a misleading combined portfolio. All runs retain curves, fills, historical decisions, filing components and exclusions. Inspect/export each trial with the same chart periods and audit as ordinary backtests.

The final test is reserved by calendar dates across snapshots and strategies in this workspace. Ordinary queued backtests cannot overlap a locked reservation. Development validation finishes before an explicit final release. Release is atomic and consumes the final test even if interrupted or failed; it cannot be run again under that protocol. Further tuning needs a new protocol and new unseen data. A protocol can be retired without deleting its record to lift its reservation.

Prior overlapping backtest attempts and trial periods, including failures and queued attempts, are disclosed. They mark the final period as previously explored. This registry does not know about external research or data already inspected outside it; downloading a dataset is not proof of blindness. Attempt counts and every trial remain visible, including interrupted/failed calculations. Multiple comparisons and repeated searching still inflate false discoveries; no p-value, deflated Sharpe, automatic pass/fail, statistical significance or trading approval is manufactured.

The workflow follows chronological evaluation principles described in [TimeSeriesSplit](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.model_selection.TimeSeriesSplit.html). Trial-count disclosure addresses the selection-bias concern in [Bailey and López de Prado, The Deflated Sharpe Ratio](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf); this implementation does not claim to implement that statistic.

Validation cannot repair fixed-list survivorship, missing departed-security returns, current classifications, annual-only fundamental coverage, ticker/share-class history or modeled publication timing. Those limits remain in the [input contract](RESEARCH_CONTRACT.md). Research results inform a separate strategy-version/environment/policy approval decision; they never activate trading.
