# Annual profitability inputs

The profitability factor is positive annual gross profit divided by fiscal year-end assets. Quality uses it directly; Quality Value also requires positive compatible annual earnings yield. Missing inputs exclude the stock from that decision. Price/action coverage alone does not establish factor eligibility.

## Evidence and completeness

Annual amounts must match the same issuer, accession, fiscal period and USD scale. Reported `GrossProfit` takes precedence when unambiguous. A derived revenue-minus-cost amount also requires compact filed-statement evidence: the reader verifies an explicit company-wide cost subtotal, a complete classified expense section, or a product/service/financing revenue partition with corresponding costs that reconciles to company revenue. Operating income is only an arithmetic check on explicitly classified expenses; it never supplies an unknown production cost or substitutes for gross profit.

A standard aggregate cost tag is insufficient by itself. Filers can apply it to purchased commodities, property operations, network access or another business stream. Absent goods/service tags do not establish a pure business or complete costs. Separately reported production depreciation, amortization, royalty, transport and financing expenses cannot disappear from the numerator. Whole-company depreciation without a supported production allocation leaves the derived value unavailable; it is not all assigned to production or administration.

An expense displayed as negative is not automatically converted to a positive cost. A supported complete expense section must establish the display convention through reconciliation. Reviewed Pulte homebuilding and finance statements also require both revenue streams, both cost streams and agreement between parent and child cost lines. Source-specific Seagate and Harris presentations distinguish explicitly allocated intangible amortization and combined engineering/administrative expenses; an unknown label cannot inherit those classifications.

Distinct revenue measures are not interchangeable. The primary statement must establish the total before cost is subtracted. Conflicting subtotals, incomplete partitions, unsupported annual columns/currencies, dimensional segments and source/period mismatches fail closed. An observed newer annual period remains present even when its supported inputs are missing, so an older favorable filing cannot silently replace it.

Recognized product/service partitions can reconcile within the combined rounding precision of the displayed monetary lines. A precision allowance does not classify or supply an expense. For example, Thermo Fisher's fiscal 2025 statement displays product and service revenues totaling $44,557 million against the $44,556 million consolidated revenue. The calculation uses the consolidated amount and both filed costs.

## Bounded source acquisition

Ordinary Download, Update and paper Prepare integrate these checks. The reader captures the accession's `FilingSummary.xml`, one primary income statement and, when useful, one quarterly-information report. It recognizes consolidated income statements that EDGAR lists as Uncategorized, and prefers a separate income statement over an OCI-only report. Standard companyfacts can omit custom concepts and dimensions.

The reader verifies accessions with structured cost or revenue leads and filings with previously reviewed layouts. For other candidates, it checks the earliest and latest relevant accessions for an issuer-specific recovery route before reading the intervening reports. An uninspected accession is disclosed as a bounded filing search. It remains possible for a valid custom layout to occur only between the samples. In a paired 2017–2026 replay, this policy avoided 1,294 accession checks and preserved every measured decision input from the exhaustive candidate.

Derived proofs, including unavailable outcomes, are cached per issuer, accession and parser and depend on retained source fingerprints. Ordinary repeat updates reuse them. Missing or changed capture dependencies cause verification again. The existing eight-starts-per-second shared SEC rate limit, document-size bound and network retry policy apply. Transport failures interrupt acquisition. Previous snapshots and saved results remain immutable.

The bounded reader does not scan every footnote or reconstruct a new profitability definition from expense-by-nature statements. Financial, utility, property and operating-company presentations are diagnosed from actual facts/tables, never solely from a sector. An unsupported presentation is a research limitation, not a claim that US-GAAP prohibits gross profit for that sector. Airline, railroad, insurance, mixed financial/industrial, depreciation-allocation and custom segment presentations can remain unavailable even when earnings or operating profit is reported.

Only the reviewed ExxonMobil and HPE supplements can read a full primary report after compact tables fail. SEC inline-viewer index links resolve only to a document in the same official accession. HPE's consolidated MD&A monetary columns must match declared October 31 fiscal years, million-dollar units, company revenue and the reported revenue/cost/gross-profit identity. Percentage and segment columns cannot supply company values. Complete quarter sequences without an explicit annual subtotal are not adopted as annual gross profit; for example, Medtronic's 2019 quarterly disclosure does not automatically fill later annual gaps.

## Definition differences and disclosed exceptions

Reported gross-profit definitions and depreciation practices can differ between issuers. Source-backed arithmetic does not establish universal accounting comparability.

- ExxonMobil's narrowly supported annual quarterly-information subtotal retains the issuer's definition involving estimated product costs and its stated sales-tax treatment. The reader requires annual headings, monetary scale and quarter/year reconciliation. It does not transplant a later filing into an earlier decision.
- HPE's explicitly reported annual gross profit precedes separately presented intangible amortization. Its reported definition is retained; this does not certify a standardized cross-company production-cost allocation.
- Costco's total-company revenue-minus-merchandise-cost route includes membership fees in revenue. It is distinct from the issuer's merchandise-only gross margin; it must not be presented as the same subtotal.
- Reviewed C.H. Robinson and Intuit product/service dimensions require every named cost and revenue stream and a complete company-wide reconciliation. Paycom's explicit administrative expense subtotal supports its separately displayed administrative depreciation when the detail adds to the subtotal.
- Target's reviewed statement explicitly identifies sales-related depreciation already included in cost of sales. The reader recognizes its separately presented remainder while requiring reconciliation of the entire expense section. [Target accounting policy](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/27419/000002741926000016/tgt-20260131.htm)
- Source-reviewed custom production-cost concepts are scoped to the specific issuer. They are not general rules for similarly named tags.

These are exploratory annual metrics. The decision audit counts missing, nonpositive and usable inputs separately. Research displays availability for the selected result interval and warns about selection bias. In the fixed historical comparison, the factor was available for 121,921 of 259,691 stock/date observations (46.95%). Coverage varied widely by financial presentation. Current or last-known sector labels describe that exposure and do not establish the reason for an individual exclusion.
