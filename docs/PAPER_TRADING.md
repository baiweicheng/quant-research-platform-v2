# Paper execution contract

This release supports Alpaca **paper only**, long US-listed stocks, fractional eligible instruments, no borrowing and no live endpoint. Research results are separate from broker records. Use a dedicated account; external positions, open orders, ambiguous transfers, short exposure, blocked accounts and cash discrepancies stop affected work. The configured initial budget is a plan, not a request to reset or fund an account.

## Approvals and the weekly workflow

Portfolio → Paper portfolio refreshes the broker account read-only. Select a versioned strategy, current stock universe, historical start, risk policy and manual/automatic mode. Suggested limits are 25 names, 5% per stock, 25% per sector, 2% cash and a 3% price-change band; saving them does not approve trading. Explicit paper approval binds the account identity, strategy version, universe and policy fingerprint. Automatic execution is an additional explicit permission. Revocation stops future submissions. Neither paper approval nor backtest completion confers live approval.

Prepare weekly paper review reconciles the account, downloads completed-session raw prices/actions, attaches required annual SEC inputs, ranks stocks, constructs constrained targets and freezes a read-only plan. Full inputs, filing components, exclusions, reference prices, maximum share quantities and dollar bounds are saved. Changed captured responses produce a new immutable snapshot; the previous review is not rewritten. In manual mode, a separate acknowledgement approves that specific rebalance. Material account/input changes require a new review.

Manual is the initial mode. Automatic mode can prepare after the week's final exchange session close plus the data-availability delay and schedule the following regular session only with matching automatic permission. The same preparation and execution functions serve both modes. The worker must remain running through downloads, execution, fills and any cash wait. A stopped Mac does not execute jobs. A missed execution session does not generate a stale catch-up order. Longer-running scheduling, backup/restore and clean-machine packaging remain operational milestones.

## Order construction and lifecycle

- Orders use fractional **DAY limit quantities**, regular hours only, and active/tradable/fractionable US-equity eligibility. Current IEX bid/ask quotes are required; IEX is one venue, not a consolidated NBBO. Quote receipt age is bounded by 60 seconds with at most 2 seconds clock skew; missing/crossed/wider-than-2% quotes hold submission while the worker waits for valid inputs within the execution window. A previously blocked quote check can explicitly resume the same frozen plan, retaining its confirmed fills; other account/order changes require a fresh review. The quote must remain within the approved reference-price band. Limit prices use a 0.1% adverse allowance, standard supported price increments, and conservative rounding.
- Preview share quantities have a 0.1% sizing cushion. Buys never exceed preview shares, preview dollars, remaining target/name/sector capacity, or conservative cash after the reserve and a $1 fee buffer. They may shrink within these disclosed bounds. Target caps are not continuous market-price guarantees; prices can drift after a check or fill. Limits are never relaxed to force investment.
- Reductions precede purchases. Filled sale proceeds remain reserved at least T+1, additionally excluding exchange closures and federal bank holidays. This is a conservative funding convention, not a certification of every broker's settlement rules. Broker restrictions may be stricter. The plan's continuation window includes this funding session.
- Open orders are reconciled, never treated as completed fills. Only one new order proceeds at a time; partial fills wait for broker resolution. An unavailable quote holds that stock; other approved targets can proceed after each confirmed fill. Pending quote reasons remain visible, and no target is silently removed or repriced outside its approved band. Rejected/canceled/expired/replaced orders stop the plan and require reviewing actual positions before a fresh plan. Remainders are never blindly resubmitted.
- Each submission has a durable unique client reference, persisted before one HTTP attempt. Timeout/server/ambiguous responses are reconciled by that reference, never blindly posted again. Unknown outcomes block new plans until broker records resolve them. Restart recovery retains orders, fills, plans and approvals. An interrupted preparation task can be rerun; an uncertain order cannot be treated as absent.
- Cash and positions are compared with the frozen account plus confirmed fills; external changes stop further submissions. Buying power/margin multipliers cannot fund the planner. The broker remains the authority for actual fills and restrictions.

Stop future submissions pauses execution. Stop & cancel requests cancellation of Aperture-owned orders only; it neither reverses fills nor cancels manually entered orders. Cancellation can race a fill, so reconcile to a terminal status. Stopping/revoking still preserves the order audit. These safeguards assume one dedicated account writer; do not trade concurrently through another app.

## Actual-account reporting

The prominent portfolio value is the last reconciled broker equity, with its timestamp. Daily performance is a separate completed-session series. Tracking starts with the first completed session captured for that account; pre-use broker seed history is excluded. Observations accumulate locally, while each refresh requests the latest year from the broker; raw history and activity responses are archived privately. A new account gets a separate baseline and no inherited strategy approval.

Cash deposits/withdrawals/cash journals are removed using an **end-of-day external-flow approximation**, not exact intraday time-weighted performance. Unsupported activity, resets and conflicting/nonpositive funded paths prevent a manufactured return. Missing daily account valuations suppress annualized volatility. SPY requires complete matching daily raw prices/actions; splits, dividend entitlements and pay-date close reinvestment are handled independently of research's next-open benchmark model. Missing comparison coverage is unavailable, not filled forward. All chart periods share dates with their metrics.

Alpaca states that its paper simulator does **not credit dividends** and omits other live effects. Reported broker equity is kept authoritative; no imaginary cash is added. SPY includes dividend entitlements, so its gap versus paper equity includes this simulator difference and is not pure strategy attribution. Paper fills also do not prove live liquidity, queue position, costs or capacity.

## First-use handover

Use Data for captured prices and optional SEC inputs; Research for backtests/comparisons and frozen validation; Portfolio for account setup, explicit paper approval and weekly operation; Activity for durable task progress. Keys/contact stay in ignored local configuration. A new paper account needs new credentials, a new read-only check, a fresh account baseline and explicit approval. Test-only state is kept outside the normal database and its approvals must be revoked after UAT.
