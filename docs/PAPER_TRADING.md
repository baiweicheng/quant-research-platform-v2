# Paper execution contract

This release supports Alpaca **paper only**, long US-listed stocks, fractional eligible instruments and no live endpoint. New paper settings explicitly permit margin funding, including possible overnight simulated debits, with a default 5% reference cash reserve. Research simulations and saved cash-only/legacy policies retain their no-borrowing rules. Research results are separate from broker records. Use a dedicated account; external positions, open orders, ambiguous transfers, short exposure, blocked accounts and cash discrepancies stop affected work. The configured initial budget is a plan, not a request to reset or fund an account.

## Approvals and the weekly workflow

Portfolio → Paper portfolio refreshes the broker account read-only. Save a versioned strategy, stock universe, backfill start, risk policy and manual/automatic mode. Suggested limits are 25 names, 5% per stock, 25% per sector and 5% cash; saving does not approve trading. New settings use **broker-staged Market DAY** execution. Explicit paper approval binds the account identity, strategy version, concrete universe and policy fingerprint. Automatic execution needs additional permission. Revocation stops future submissions; accepted broker orders remain active until filled or canceled. No paper approval confers live permission. The funding policy is part of the approval fingerprint; prior cash-only or limit approvals cannot authorize margin staging.

Prepare weekly paper review reconciles the account, updates completed-session prices/actions and required annual SEC inputs, ranks stocks and freezes a read-only plan. Inputs, filing components, exclusions, reference prices, fixed-dollar buys and quantity sells are retained. In manual mode **Approve & stage at Alpaca** confirms the particular rebalance, price gaps and paper-margin policy. The request itself stages the batch; it returns success only after every instruction has an explicit broker receipt. It does not require a later execution-worker tick. API and worker attempts share a process-safe order lock. Material account/input changes require a fresh review.

For S&P settings, preparation refreshes both membership sources. Additions are backfilled from the saved history start (default January 4, 2016), with unavailable inputs visible in Data. A changed roster pauses plan creation: inspect additions/removals, adopt the updated settings, and obtain a new matching approval. Membership changes never silently widen an approved trading list. Held departures stay exit-only, retaining prices and prior sector classification for reductions/capacity checks; they cannot enter buy ranks. Current signal preparation uses a rolling window of up to 740 calendar days, bounded by the saved start; historical research snapshots remain separate and immutable. No separate ordinary weekly Data download is required.

Manual is the initial mode. Prepare and approve Friday/Sunday night or after another completed close, before the intended next regular open. Keep Aperture running through downloads and submission until the plan says **all orders accepted**. Then the Mac may be off: Alpaca holds accepted orders. Acceptance is distinct from filling. On the next launch the worker automatically reads owned orders and reconciles fills, cash and positions; reopening the interface also requests a read-only check if the last check is stale. No new plan can bypass unresolved owned orders. Actual average fill, reference price and percentage difference are shown in the lifecycle table.

Automatic mode uses the same pipeline with matching explicit automatic permission, after the week's final exchange close and availability delay. It still needs a running Mac through preparation and confirmed broker acceptance. It does not wake a stopped Mac. Instructions not submitted before their intended regular open expire locally; no stale catch-up is sent. Hosting and broader operational hardening remain future milestones.

## Order construction and lifecycle

- Regular-session **Market DAY** orders have `extended_hours: false`. Buys use frozen dollar notionals; sells use fixed share quantities. Alpaca supports fractional market DAY orders where eligible. Market orders have no reference-price cap or guaranteed fill; actual shares, prices and weights can differ. DAY remainders can expire at session end.
- All instructions are sent without waiting for earlier accepted orders to fill. Active/tradable/fractionable US-equity eligibility is checked independently at submission. No regular-hours quote or legacy price band is required for this policy.
- New paper-margin funding stages buys and sells together within broker-reported regular buying power, bounded by reference sale proceeds and the target cash reserve, with $1 per-buy buffers. Planned reductions provide reference sector capacity; actual interim/final exposure can differ when orders fail or prices gap. No intended reference portfolio borrows to exceed its approved targets. Buy-first fills or failed/shortfall sells can nevertheless leave an overnight simulated debit. Reviews show estimated cash after all reference trades and the debit if buys fill first. An increased cash reserve mitigates this risk but cannot eliminate it. There is no app-dependent liquidation, sell-then-buy continuation or automatic transfer while the Mac is off.
- Saved cash-only Market DAY settings retain their original behavior: existing spendable cash funds buys and sale-dependent amounts remain deferred. Changing funding requires saving settings and new matching approval. Insufficient buying power or capacity can still reduce/defer a new batch; inspect the actual instructions before approval.
- Each instruction has a durable client ID saved before one HTTP attempt. Timeout/server/ambiguous outcomes are looked up by that ID; no blind repost or assumed remainder is sent. Explicit broker receipts are required before the app reports the whole batch staged. A partially staged batch must be inspected before shutdown.
- Reconciliation distinguishes accepted, partial, filled, canceled, expired, rejected and uncertain states. Completion requires positive confirmed fill quantities/prices plus matching cash/positions. Failed or inconsistent records require attention. **Review remaining trades** prepares a fresh next-session review using updated prices, holdings and actual fills after outstanding broker orders resolve; it does not clone or blindly resend the previous orders. Records survive restarts and revocation. External account changes stop further submissions.

**Legacy settings:** saved `paper-limits-v1` configurations keep their original fingerprint and regular-hours limit/quote/settlement behavior. Editing and saving settings switches to Market DAY and requires new matching approval; it never inherits approval for the new policy. Legacy execution requires the worker through fills and conservative T+1 funding waits. Its price band is not a Market DAY safeguard.

Stop future submissions pauses new execution. Stop & cancel requests cancellation of Aperture-owned orders only; neither action reverses fills or cancels unrelated manual orders. Cancellation can race a fill, so reconcile to a terminal state. Closing Aperture or revoking approval does not cancel accepted broker orders. Use one dedicated account writer and do not trade concurrently through another app.

## Actual-account reporting

The prominent portfolio value is the last reconciled broker equity, with its timestamp. Daily performance is a separate completed-session series. Tracking starts with the first completed session captured for that account; pre-use broker seed history is excluded. Observations accumulate locally, while each refresh requests the latest year from the broker; raw history and activity responses are archived privately. A new account gets a separate baseline and no inherited strategy approval.

Cash deposits/withdrawals/cash journals are removed using an **end-of-day external-flow approximation**, not exact intraday time-weighted performance. Unsupported activity, resets and conflicting/nonpositive funded paths prevent a manufactured return. Missing daily account valuations suppress annualized volatility. SPY requires complete matching daily raw prices/actions; splits, dividend entitlements and pay-date close reinvestment are handled independently of research's next-open benchmark model. Missing comparison coverage is unavailable, not filled forward. All chart periods share dates with their metrics.

Alpaca states that its paper simulator does **not credit dividends** and omits other live effects. Reported broker equity is kept authoritative; no imaginary cash is added. SPY includes dividend entitlements, so its gap versus paper equity includes this simulator difference and is not pure strategy attribution. Paper fills also do not prove live liquidity, queue position, costs or capacity.

## First-use handover

Use Data for captured prices and optional SEC inputs; Research for backtests/comparisons and frozen validation; Portfolio for account setup, explicit paper approval and weekly operation; Activity for durable task progress. Keys/contact stay in ignored local configuration. A new paper account needs new credentials, a new read-only check, a fresh account baseline and explicit approval. Test-only state is kept outside the normal database and its approvals must be revoked after UAT.
