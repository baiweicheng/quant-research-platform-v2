# Paper execution contract

This release supports Alpaca **paper only**, long US-listed stocks, fractional eligible instruments and no live endpoint. New paper settings explicitly permit margin funding, including possible overnight simulated debits, with a default 5% reference cash reserve. Research simulations and saved cash-only/legacy policies retain their no-borrowing rules. Research results are separate from broker records. Use a dedicated account; external positions, open orders, ambiguous transfers, short exposure, blocked accounts and cash discrepancies stop affected work. The configured initial budget is a plan, not a request to reset or fund an account.

## Approvals and rebalance timing

Portfolio → Paper portfolio refreshes the broker account read-only. Save a versioned strategy, stock universe, backfill start, risk policy and manual/automatic mode. Suggested limits are 25 names, 5% per stock, 25% per sector and 5% cash; saving does not approve trading. New settings use **broker-staged Market DAY** execution. Explicit paper approval binds the account identity, strategy version, concrete universe and policy fingerprint. Automatic execution needs additional permission. Revocation stops future submissions; accepted broker orders remain active until filled or canceled. No paper approval confers live permission. The funding policy is part of the approval fingerprint; prior cash-only or limit approvals cannot authorize margin staging.

**Prepare weekly paper review** or **Prepare month-end paper review** reconciles the account, updates completed-session market data and SEC filings, and freezes a read-only plan. Ranking and sizing share the research strategy rules. Weekly Earnings Reaction Tracker, Earnings and Market Capture, Earnings and Pullback Rotation, and Liquidity and Volatility Rotation read comparable periodic income from the snapshot’s saved SEC filings. Liquidity and Breadth uses price and volume signals. These five weekly strategies use reconciled paper holdings for the incumbent preference. Inputs, filing components, exclusions, reference prices, fixed-dollar buys and quantity sells are retained. In manual mode **Approve & stage at Alpaca** confirms the particular rebalance, price gaps and paper-margin policy. The request itself stages the batch and returns success only after every instruction has an explicit broker receipt. API and worker attempts share a process-safe order lock. Material account or input changes require a fresh review.

All strategies in the Research library are selectable in Paper Settings. Profitable Liquidity Leaders, Session Strength and Liquidity, and Earnings and Market Regime Rotation use the final exchange session of each calendar month. The other visible strategies follow the weekly cadence. The regular preparation window opens when the period’s final session data is available and closes at the following regular open. An exchange holiday can move the decision or execution session. New paper activity requires an available strategy and a matching approval for the selected recipe, account and policy. For a saved plan from a hidden strategy, the portfolio shows a generic strategy label and retains order reconciliation and cancellation.

**Aim to rebalance at month-end.** If you miss the scheduled open, choose **Prepare month-end catch-up** after a later session's data is available. The planned regular open must be within seven calendar days of the original one, including day seven when markets are open. Catch-up is manual. It reuses a matching saved month-end target or reconstructs it with information available by that close. It rechecks current account holdings, open orders, prices and eligibility, then builds a fresh plan for manual review and approval. Targets dropped on current eligibility leave their weight in cash. A month that already completed or has confirmed fills cannot catch up again. Each plan expires at its intended regular open. If you miss the seven-day window, wait for the next month-end.

For S&P settings, preparation refreshes both membership sources. Additions are backfilled from the saved history start (default January 4, 2016), with unavailable inputs visible in Data. A changed roster pauses plan creation: inspect additions/removals, adopt the updated settings, and obtain a new matching approval. Membership changes never silently widen an approved trading list. Held departures stay exit-only, retaining prices and prior sector classification for reductions/capacity checks; they cannot enter buy ranks. Current signal preparation uses a rolling window of up to 740 calendar days, bounded by the saved start; historical research snapshots remain separate and immutable. Every normal preparation snapshot contains both market data and SEC filings; a price strategy ignores the filing portion. Unchanged price, filing and provider-response blocks are shared across snapshots. No daily full-dataset schedule is introduced. Daily account/fill records remain separate, and no separate ordinary weekly Data download is required. Coverage-limited research exclusions never relax current paper input, universe, tradability or approval checks; the ex-date research convention does not create broker cash.

Manual is the initial mode. Prepare and approve Friday/Sunday night or after another completed close, before the intended next regular open. Keep Aperture running through downloads and submission until the plan says **all orders accepted**. Then the Mac may be off: Alpaca holds accepted orders. Acceptance is distinct from filling. On the next launch the worker automatically reads owned orders and reconciles fills, cash and positions; reopening the interface also requests a read-only check if the last check is stale. No new plan can bypass unresolved owned orders. Actual average fill, reference price and percentage difference are shown in the lifecycle table.

Automatic mode uses the same pipeline with matching explicit automatic permission, after the strategy period's final exchange close and availability delay. It still needs a running Mac through preparation and confirmed broker acceptance. It does not wake a stopped Mac. Instructions not submitted before their intended regular open expire locally. Hosting and broader operational hardening remain future milestones.

## Order construction and lifecycle

- Regular-session **Market DAY** orders have `extended_hours: false`. Buys use frozen dollar notionals; sells use fixed share quantities. Alpaca supports fractional market DAY orders where eligible. Market orders have no reference-price cap or guaranteed fill; actual shares, prices and weights can differ. DAY remainders can expire at session end.
- All instructions are sent without waiting for earlier accepted orders to fill. Active/tradable/fractionable US-equity eligibility is checked independently at submission. No regular-hours quote or legacy price band is required for this policy.
- New paper-margin funding stages buys and sells together within broker-reported regular buying power, bounded by reference sale proceeds and the target cash reserve, with $1 per-buy buffers. Planned reductions provide reference sector capacity; actual interim/final exposure can differ when orders fail or prices gap. No intended reference portfolio borrows to exceed its approved targets. Buy-first fills or failed/shortfall sells can nevertheless leave an overnight simulated debit. Reviews show estimated cash after all reference trades and the debit if buys fill first. An increased cash reserve mitigates this risk but cannot eliminate it. There is no app-dependent liquidation, sell-then-buy continuation or automatic transfer while the Mac is off.
- Saved cash-only Market DAY settings retain their original behavior: existing spendable cash funds buys and sale-dependent amounts remain deferred. Changing funding requires saving settings and new matching approval. Insufficient buying power or capacity can still reduce/defer a new batch; inspect the actual instructions before approval.
- Each instruction has a durable client ID saved before the HTTP attempt. If a temporary read or pacing failure happens before submission, staging can resume from the saved receipts before the same opening deadline. An ambiguous submission must be reconciled by client ID. Keep Aperture open until the plan confirms broker acceptance for the entire batch. An empty review completes locally when no trades are needed and shows deferred amounts when funding or capacity prevented orders.
- The app paces trading API requests across its worker and UI, reconciles outstanding orders in batches, and backs off when Alpaca throttles reads. Each Market DAY submission checks Alpaca's next regular open and leaves at least 10 seconds before that open for delivery. If the deadline passes, previously accepted orders stay at Alpaca and the unsent balance needs a fresh review.
- Reconciliation requires positive confirmed fill quantities and prices plus matching cash and positions. **Confirm broker adjustments** is available when the broker supplies activity references that explain a discrepancy. Verify the listed activities and changes at Alpaca before confirming. Aperture reads the account again and accepts the confirmation only if the documented review still matches. Outstanding orders or unexplained differences keep execution blocked.
- **Review remaining trades** can prepare a manual weekly recovery for a remaining regular open in the same execution week once the affected account and orders reconcile. It preserves the original weekly targets, sizes unfinished instructions against current holdings and completed-session prices, and requires separate approval for the new plan.
- Weekly S&P 500 preparation checks current membership and preserves roster approval. The review identifies unheld symbols omitted for unavailable verified inputs or broker eligibility. An ineligible new target's weight stays in cash. Unverified SPY or held-position inputs must be resolved before approval.

**Legacy settings:** saved `paper-limits-v1` configurations keep their original fingerprint and regular-hours limit/quote/settlement behavior. Editing and saving settings switches to Market DAY and requires new matching approval; it never inherits approval for the new policy. Legacy execution requires the worker through fills and conservative T+1 funding waits. Its price band is not a Market DAY safeguard.

**Stop future submissions** pauses new execution. **Stop & cancel Aperture orders** also queues broker cancellation, which the worker services independently of downloads and research. Keep Aperture open and check the cancellation status until the broker confirms the order outcomes. A fill can win the race, in which case the actual fill is retained. Closing Aperture or revoking approval leaves accepted orders at the broker. Use one dedicated account writer.

If submission remains uncertain, refresh the paper account and check the client reference and account directly at Alpaca. Use **Review uncertain order** in the order lifecycle when Alpaca has no matching record. Aperture requires the same account, repeated absence checks at least 30 seconds apart, a five-minute buffer after the planned DAY execution window, and cash and positions that match confirmed fills. The reviewed client reference stays in history. Prepare a fresh plan for any remaining trades. Contact Alpaca if the reference or account balance is still ambiguous.

## Actual-account reporting

The prominent portfolio value is the last reconciled broker equity, with its timestamp. Daily performance is a separate completed-session series. Tracking starts with the first completed session captured for that account; pre-use broker seed history is excluded. Observations accumulate locally, while each refresh requests the latest year from the broker; raw history and activity responses are archived privately. A new account gets a separate baseline and no inherited strategy approval.

Cash deposits/withdrawals/cash journals are removed using an **end-of-day external-flow approximation**, not exact intraday time-weighted performance. Unsupported activity, resets and conflicting/nonpositive funded paths prevent a manufactured return. Missing daily account valuations suppress annualized volatility. SPY requires complete matching daily raw prices/actions; splits, dividend entitlements and pay-date close reinvestment are handled independently of research's next-open benchmark model. Missing comparison coverage is unavailable, not filled forward. All chart periods share dates with their metrics.

Alpaca states that its paper simulator does **not credit dividends** and omits other live effects. Reported broker equity is kept authoritative; no imaginary cash is added. SPY includes dividend entitlements, so its gap versus paper equity includes this simulator difference and is not pure strategy attribution. Paper fills also do not prove live liquidity, queue position, costs or capacity.

## First-use handover

Use Data for captured prices and optional SEC inputs; Research for backtests/comparisons and frozen validation; Portfolio for account setup, explicit paper approval and weekly operation; Activity for durable task progress. Keys/contact stay in ignored local configuration. A new paper account needs new credentials, a new read-only check, a fresh account baseline and explicit approval. Test-only state is kept outside the normal database and its approvals must be revoked after UAT.
