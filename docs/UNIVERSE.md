# S&P 500 universe contract

Data defaults to **S&P 500 — current and historical members**, with optional additional symbols and SPY reserved for benchmarking. One download takes the deduplicated union of today's roster and every historical member recorded during the selected price period, including departures. An index can contain more than 500 securities because multiple share classes are distinct listings. Extra symbols never replace the core roster. An explicit Custom list only mode remains available and is labelled exploratory.

The interface has one **Refresh S&P 500 membership** action for both sources. Current and historical captures remain distinct internally because one records a roster observed now and the other records dated changes. Source links, capture times, last recorded change and additions/removals are available under **Membership sources and changes**. The historical headline counts all unique symbols in the archive, rather than presenting its final roster count as the total history.

Price dates can extend through the latest completed session even when membership history is behind. Each combined dataset freezes both captures and the relevant historical timeline. Its warning reports supported backtest membership dates, or explicitly states there is no historical overlap. Backtests require dated membership and required-member prices throughout their selected test period. Current reviews use the observed-current roster, subject to existing freshness checks; current-only names cannot enter earlier historical ranks. Captured current sectors apply only to current reviews; historical classifications remain Unknown.

Current members with no returned prices in an older requested period remain listed with zero bars and explicit coverage, including after dataset reload. They cannot enter an earlier historical rank. A missing historical member, requested supplement or benchmark with no bars still stops ingestion, and a missing in-period required-member session stops validation. Provider identity, corporate-action and split checks remain in force. Requests retain the 1,500-security and 4,000,000-bar bounds and 2016 market-data start.

## Current membership and refresh

Current membership is read from the [Wikipedia constituent table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_S%26P_500_companies), including company name, current sector and CIK. This public secondary source is not a licensed index feed. The parser requires the expected columns, plausible security count, unique valid symbols and complete identity fields. Source changes or download failures preserve existing captures and fail the requested refresh. No different provider is silently substituted.

Data → Refresh S&P 500 membership saves an immutable current capture, source URL, SHA-256 digest, retrieval timestamp, and additions/removals relative to the previous capture, then refreshes the historical source. A failed two-source refresh reports incompleteness and retains any successful capture; it publishes no price dataset. The original responses are retained locally. Each new S&P download checks both capture ages and refreshes each if over 24 hours old. Refresh is also available without Alpaca keys. Navigation performs no provider downloads. This is an on-demand refresh, with no new scheduled task or always-on guarantee.

The captured roster is frozen into each price dataset and its source fingerprint. Refreshing membership does not change old datasets, results or strategy approvals. A current roster supports a current review using the latest completed prices; this explicitly means selection from the roster observed now. **Legacy current-only price snapshots cannot run historical backtests**, even when they contain many years of warm-up prices. Download the combined S&P option to include dated membership. Older CSV/fixed-list snapshots retain their original exploratory interpretation; legacy current-only/historical-only API requests remain compatible.

## Historical membership

The unified refresh also reads [fja05680's historical roster archive](https://github.com/fja05680/sp500). Its author maintains an original history with later changes and explicitly describes completeness and delisted-price limitations. These are third-party retrospective rosters, **not a certified as-known archive**. At verification on November 3, 2026, it contained 2,720 effective-dated rosters from January 2, 1996 through August 18, 2026. Market ingestion itself starts in 2016. The final date is the last recorded change, not an inherent expiry. A roster continues until the next change if the change history is verified complete through the requested date. In this captured archive later changes are demonstrably missing: [S&P announced September 21 changes](https://press.spglobal.com/2026-09-04-Bloom-Energy,-Illumina,-and-Everpure-Set-to-Join-S-P-500-Others-to-Join-S-P-100,-S-P-MidCap-400,-and-S-P-SmallCap-600). The app conservatively bounds supported backtests at the last recorded row until later coverage is supplied. Refreshing an unchanged file does not establish later completeness. This interface change does not reconstruct missing changes.

The combined S&P download applies the following historical controls (legacy historical-only requests retain their stricter acquisition bounds):

1. Separates requested price dates from the supported historical-membership interval. A historical-only API request still checks both requested boundaries before downloading; the combined request may acquire newer prices without treating them as later membership evidence.
2. Includes the roster already in effect at the start of the overlapping historical interval and every recorded change through its end, including stocks that subsequently departed, plus today's roster. Supplemental names and SPY are added and deduplicated.
3. Captures active and inactive listing metadata. Missing or conflicting ticker identities stop ingestion; an inactive stock can provide historical prices without being eligible for execution.
4. Requests raw bars, actions and independent split-adjusted bars in batches of at most 100 symbols. The bounded limit is 1,500 securities including SPY and 4,000,000 daily bars. It retains the existing action/accounting checks.
5. Requires a price on every in-period session for each then-member. It stops on an unavailable member instead of treating missing data as a reason to drop that member. Prices before a stock joins may be used for signal warm-up, but the stock cannot be selected before its effective membership date.
6. Filters the cross-section on every decision date **before** factor ranks and target weights. An effective-date roster replaces the prior roster starting on that date; removal/re-entry are supported. Execution still follows the next-session model. A missing held-stock price remains a hard failure; no delisting exit or recovery is invented.
7. Freezes the relevant timeline, capture time, source digest, supplements and membership snapshot ID into the dataset and exported research audit. SEC enrichment preserves it.

The file has effective dates but lacks original announcement timestamps and verified permanent security identifiers. Retrieval time is retained separately and is never passed off as original announcement time. Ticker reuse, retrospective corrections, source omissions, delisted data, historic share classes and corporate actions remain material uncertainties. The public archive's dates are used only for explicitly labelled retrospective research. **No result is certified survivorship-safe or fully point-in-time correct.** A validated vintage/security-master archive plus complete departed-security prices/actions would be needed for that stronger claim.

Historical sectors are Unknown rather than backfilled from today's GICS classification. They share the configured sector cap; this can keep substantial cash. Extra stocks form an explicitly fixed supplement, which can introduce selection/survivorship bias even when the S&P core varies correctly over time.

## Paper boundaries

Paper settings retain a concrete frozen symbol list and account/strategy/policy fingerprint. The current captured S&P roster is offered as an editable starting list when settings have not yet been saved. Subsequent membership refreshes never widen an existing approved paper universe. Changing its symbols requires a new matching approval. Asset tradability is checked independently by paper planning/submission; historical ingestion of an inactive listing cannot authorize an order.

No strategies are activated and no orders are submitted by membership refresh or market downloads.
