# S&P 500 universe contract

Data defaults to **S&P 500 — current and historical members**, as the default. The other choices are **S&P 500 + custom list** and **Custom list only**, with matching default dataset names. Manually entered names are preserved when switching. SPY is reserved for benchmarking; the default history start is **2016-01-04**, the first supported US session in Alpaca's history since 2016. Individual securities may begin later. One download takes the deduplicated union of today's roster and every historical member recorded during the selected price period, including departures. An index can contain more than 500 securities because multiple share classes are distinct listings. Extra symbols never replace the core roster. Custom lists remain explicitly exploratory.

The interface has one **Refresh S&P 500 membership** action for both sources. Current and historical captures remain distinct internally because one records a roster observed now and the other records dated changes. Source links, capture times, last recorded change and additions/removals are available under **Membership sources and changes**. The historical headline counts all unique symbols in the archive, rather than presenting its final roster count as the total history.

Price dates can extend through the latest completed session even when membership history is behind. Each combined dataset freezes both captures and the relevant historical timeline. Its warning reports supported backtest membership dates, or explicitly states there is no historical overlap. Backtests require dated membership and required-member prices throughout their selected test period. Current reviews use the observed-current roster, subject to existing freshness checks; current-only names cannot enter earlier historical ranks. Captured current sectors apply only to current reviews; historical classifications remain Unknown.

Current members with no returned prices in an older requested period remain listed with zero bars and explicit coverage, including after dataset reload. They cannot enter an earlier historical rank. Combined downloads retain requested names and available raw bars even when a security identity, corporate action, split cross-check or required price is unresolved. Per-stock coverage reasons and validation issues remain visible; affected ranks, reviews and backtests hard-fail rather than dropping members. A flagged SPY series cannot produce a benchmark. Legacy strict/custom acquisition still stops on unsupported inputs. Requests retain the 1,500-security and 4,000,000-bar bounds and 2016 market-data start.

## Current membership and refresh

Current membership is read from the [Wikipedia constituent table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_S%26P_500_companies), including company name, current sector, CIK and reported date added. This public secondary source is not a licensed index feed. The parser requires the expected columns, plausible security count, unique valid symbols and complete identity fields. Source changes or download failures preserve existing captures and fail the requested refresh. No different provider is silently substituted.

Data → Refresh S&P 500 membership saves an immutable current capture, source URL, SHA-256 digest, retrieval timestamp, and additions/removals relative to the previous capture, then refreshes the historical source. A failed two-source refresh reports incompleteness and retains any successful capture; it publishes no price dataset. The original responses are retained locally. New S&P downloads refresh captures older than 24 hours or whose verified coverage trails a new completed session; yesterday's capture cannot leave today's completed backtest unsupported merely because it is less than 24 hours old. Research dataset updates explicitly refresh both sources. Refresh is also available without Alpaca keys. Navigation performs no provider downloads. This is on-demand, with no new scheduled task or always-on guarantee.

The captured roster is frozen into each price dataset and its source fingerprint. Refreshing membership does not change old datasets, results or strategy approvals. A current roster supports a current review using the latest completed prices; this explicitly means selection from the roster observed now. **Legacy current-only price snapshots cannot run historical backtests**, even when they contain many years of warm-up prices. Download the combined S&P option to include dated membership. Older CSV/fixed-list snapshots retain their original exploratory interpretation; legacy current-only/historical-only API requests remain compatible.

## Historical membership

The unified refresh reads [fja05680's historical roster archive](https://github.com/fja05680/sp500) and extends its tail using [S&P's announcement archive](https://press.spglobal.com/index.php?keywords=S%26P+500&l=100&s=2429). These remain retrospective research rosters, **not a certified as-known archive**. On November 4, 2026 the base file still ended August 18, but the reconstructed timeline reconciled exactly to the observed 503-security roster, with coverage verified through the November 4 completed session. Market ingestion starts in 2016.

The refresh scans bounded, paginated primary release listings back to 45 days before the historical anchor, parses S&P 500 additions/deletions using their effective dates, applies same-day changes together, and demands exact reconciliation with the separately observed current roster. It records source URLs, response hashes/raw evidence and event dates. Future-effective announcements do not enter eligibility early. Unsupported formats, conflicting transitions, insufficient listing depth or unexplained roster differences fail the refresh and preserve prior captures. A small explicitly reviewed ticker-change ledger handles PSKY→SKYD on October 6, based on the [issuer's completed-transaction announcement](https://ir.skydance.com/news-releases/news-release-details/paramount-completes-acquisition-warner-bros-discovery-creating); it neither splices prices nor accounts for the merger. Unexplained future ticker changes block reconciliation until supported.

Primary evidence includes [September 21 substitutions](https://press.spglobal.com/2026-09-04-Bloom-Energy,-Illumina,-and-Everpure-Set-to-Join-S-P-500-Others-to-Join-S-P-100,-S-P-MidCap-400,-and-S-P-SmallCap-600), [Vylor's October 1 addition and October 6 deletions/addition](https://press.spglobal.com/2026-10-01-Vylor-Added-to-the-S-P-500-Twilio-Set-to-Join-S-P-500-Others-to-Join-S-P-MidCap-400-and-S-P-SmallCap-600), and [Okta replacing Kenvue on November 4](https://press.spglobal.com/2026-10-30-Okta-Set-to-Join-S-P-500-Others-to-Join-S-P-MidCap-400-and-S-P-SmallCap-600). October 30 was Okta's announcement date, not its entry date; the secondary change table had confused them. Vylor's temporary addition produces 504 securities until the October 6 changes; do not force every intermediate roster to 503.

Data distinguishes **last effective change**, **capture time**, and **verified-through coverage**. An unchanged roster may carry forward through verified coverage; the last event is not an expiry. Primary announcements plus endpoint reconciliation strengthen the free-source tail but cannot certify absence of every unreported round-trip change, original knowledge times or permanent security identities. Existing datasets freeze their old timelines: use [Update dataset](SNAPSHOTS.md) to obtain a new snapshot with repaired membership and current prices/filings, without re-downloading all existing price history.

The combined S&P download applies the following historical controls (legacy historical-only requests retain their stricter acquisition bounds):

1. Separates requested price dates from the supported historical-membership interval. A historical-only API request still checks both requested boundaries before downloading; the combined request may acquire newer prices without treating them as later membership evidence.
2. Includes the roster already in effect at the start of the overlapping historical interval and every recorded change through its end, including stocks that subsequently departed, plus today's roster. Supplemental names and SPY are added and deduplicated.
3. Captures active/inactive listing evidence, dated provider `asof` queries and source-linked original quote aliases. Rename chains follow event chronology and indexed lifetimes to avoid unrelated reused tickers. Dated queries strengthen evidence; vendor raw-symbol fallback and permanent historical identities remain uncertified. Conflicting identities/actions remain dated issues, not guessed resolutions.
4. Requests raw bars, corporate actions and a separate split-adjusted series in batches of at most 100 symbols. Missing foreign-dividend currency is cross-checked against USD dividend-adjusted evidence rather than assumed. The bounded limit is 1,500 securities including SPY and 4,000,000 daily bars. Known vendor split/spin-off conflicts are quarantined with source links.
5. Strict full-index mode requires every then-member's in-period prices and supported inputs. The separately approved coverage-limited mode excludes unavailable/unverified inputs using the whole test window, records each stock/reason and discloses resulting survivorship/selection bias. It is an exploratory subset, not a complete index result. A stock's missing warm-up history can still make it ineligible for a signal. Neither mode invents missing held prices or terminal proceeds.
6. Filters the cross-section on every decision date **before** factor ranks and target weights. An effective-date roster replaces the prior roster starting on that date; removal/re-entry are supported. Execution still follows the next-session model. A missing held-stock price remains a hard failure; no delisting exit or recovery is invented.
7. Freezes the relevant timeline, capture time, source digest, supplements and membership snapshot ID into the dataset and exported research audit. SEC enrichment preserves it.

The file has effective dates but lacks original announcement timestamps and verified permanent security identifiers. Retrieval time is retained separately and is never passed off as original announcement time. Ticker reuse, retrospective corrections, source omissions, delisted data, historic share classes and corporate actions remain material uncertainties. The public archive's dates are used only for explicitly labelled retrospective research. **No result is certified survivorship-safe or fully point-in-time correct.** A validated vintage/security-master archive plus complete departed-security prices/actions would be needed for that stronger claim.

Historical sectors are Unknown rather than backfilled from today's GICS classification. They share the configured sector cap; this can keep substantial cash. Extra stocks form an explicitly fixed supplement, which can introduce selection/survivorship bias even when the S&P core varies correctly over time.

## Paper boundaries

Paper settings retain a concrete frozen symbol list and account/strategy/policy fingerprint. The same three universe choices are available, with S&P only as default. Weekly preparation refreshes membership and backfills additions from the saved history start. Changes pause plan creation for explicit roster adoption and new matching approval. Held departures remain exit-only and cannot enter buy ranks. Refreshes never silently widen an approved paper universe. Asset tradability is checked independently by paper planning/submission; historical ingestion of an inactive listing cannot authorize an order.

No strategies are activated and no orders are submitted by membership refresh or market downloads.
